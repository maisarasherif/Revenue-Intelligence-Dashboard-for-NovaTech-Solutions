Marketing Funnel
The sheet provides a comprehensive overview of marketing campaign performance, tracking leads, conversion rates, revenue attribution, and ROI across multiple campaigns.

Despite generating 2,240 total leads and $1,127,223 in attributed revenue, the overall Campaign ROI is a staggering -90.9% against a total spend of $12,359,497.

NovaPulse Launch stands out with the highest closed-won conversion rate at 33.7%, far exceeding the overall rate of 18.4%, while NovaEdge Awareness trails at just 5.1%.

The marketing funnel shows significant drop-off, narrowing from 2,240 prospects to 1,371 qualified leads.

Digital Retarget generated the most leads at 419 but achieved only a 16.2% conversion rate, suggesting quantity does not guarantee quality.
